Block 1: Every day, humans throw away enough food to feed two billion people, and most of it never even reaches a plate.

Block 2: We blame picky eaters and overfull fridges, but the biggest losses happen long before you ever see the food on a shelf.

Block 3: Nearly forty percent of waste in poorer countries happens at the farm, where crops rot waiting for trucks that never come.

Block 4: Rich countries flipped the problem, because their food survives the long journey and then dies in supermarkets chasing perfect-looking produce.

Block 5: Here is the twist, fixing trucks and fridges would cut far more waste than every household campaign ever launched combined.

Block 6: So the fight against food waste is not in your kitchen, it is in the boring machinery that moves dinner around.
